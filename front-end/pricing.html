QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How does LeadRevive work?

LeadRevive analyzes customer conversations, identifies high-intent leads, detects potential lost leads, and recommends the next follow-up action.

Does LeadRevive automatically send messages?

In the initial MVP, LeadRevive provides an AI-generated recommendation and requires human approval before a follow-up is sent.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade your plan when your lead volume or AI usage increases.

What happens when I reach my AI usage limit?

You can upgrade to a higher plan when you need additional conversation analysis or AI follow-up usage.

Is human approval required?

Yes. The initial LeadRevive workflow keeps a human in the loop so you can review and approve AI recommendations before sending them.

What platforms will LeadRevive support?

Platform integrations will be introduced gradually. The MVP focuses first on analyzing conversations and generating useful follow-up recommendations.
